Can you hit a 160 km/h fastball?

The pitcher throws 100 pitches. Your job: knock them out of the park.
Tap the spot where the ball will cross the plate, at exactly the right moment. Square it up and the ball soars into the night sky — clear the fence and the crowd erupts with cheers and fireworks.

■ PITCHES THAT KEEP YOU GUESSING
• Fastballs up to 163 km/h (101 mph)
• Sliders, curveballs, shuuto (screwballs), forkballs and slow balls
• Breaking balls that snap late — but every pitch is a strike
• The pitcher changes his rhythm every time, with the occasional quick pitch

■ PHYSICS-BASED HITTING
• Swing early to pull the ball, late to go the other way
• Hit just under the ball to lift it, over it for a grounder
• Distance is calculated from exit speed, launch angle and direction
• After every pitch you see where the ball crossed, where you tapped, and how many milliseconds early or late you were

■ BALLPARK ATMOSPHERE
• Night game with trumpet-and-drum cheering and the whoosh of every pitch
• Groans when you whiff, cheers and fireworks when you go deep
• Your phone vibrates on contact

■ EASY TO PLAY
• Three levels: 160km/h (real speed), Pro and Rookie
• Automatic timing calibration for your phone's screen and touch delay
• Earn a title based on home runs, hits, batting average and longest drive
• Share your results to X, LINE and more
• Watch an ad after the game for 10 bonus pitches in extra innings
• Plays offline (ads and sharing need a connection)

How many can you hit out of 100? Step up to the plate.
